Admission Information

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • Class I: Age 5+ years
  • Class VI: Class V pass or equivalent
  • Class IX: Class VIII pass or equivalent
  • Class XI: Class X pass with relevant stream
  • No age bar for transfers

📅 Important Dates

  • Registration: January - March
  • Entrance Test: April - May
  • Interview: May - June
  • Admission Decision: June - July
  • Session Starts: July

📄 Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Report Card
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • Medical Certificate
  • Photographs (2x2 & 1x1)
  • ID Proof

💰 Fee Structure

  • Primary (I-V): ₹50,000 - 70,000/year
  • Middle (VI-VIII): ₹60,000 - 80,000/year
  • High School (IX-X): ₹70,000 - 90,000/year
  • Senior (XI-XII): ₹80,000 - 1,00,000/year
  • Scholarships available for meritorious students

Frequently Asked Questions

When does admission process start? +

Admission process typically starts in January and continues through June. Early registration is recommended for better class options.

Is there an entrance examination? +

Yes, entrance examinations are conducted for Class 6 onwards. For Class 1, admission is based on interaction and basic readiness assessment.

What about merit scholarships? +

We offer merit scholarships based on entrance test performance and academic records. Up to 50% fee waiver is available for top performers.

Can transfer students apply? +

Yes, students from other schools can apply for admission. Transfer certificates and previous academic records are required.

What is the fee payment schedule? +

Fees can be paid annually, semi-annually, or quarterly. Installment plans are available for families needing financial support.
